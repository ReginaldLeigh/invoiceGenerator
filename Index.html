2 Match your columns

Enter the column letter (A, B, … AA) for each field. Tip: click a box below, then click a column letter in the preview above.

Accepted tax codes (any capitalisation): EXEMPT, GST, HST, NSHST, ONHST, PEI, QST, or the French TPS, TVH, NETVH, ONTVH, IPE. A blank or unknown code is invoiced as EXEMPT, with a note.

Group line items by · optional

Lines are listed under a heading for each value in this column, e.g. Project Number: P-1001. Totals are not affected. The heading label defaults to that column's header.

Extra details · optional, up to 10

Each extra detail is printed as a small line under its line item, e.g. Location: Studio A. Enter the column letter; the label defaults to that column's header.
